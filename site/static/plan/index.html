BUSINESS PLAN · NOVEMBER 2026

Share the idle.
Survive the crunch.

Apiary is a member-owned pool of LLM capacity. Members contribute what sits idle, earn credit for what they actually deliver, and draw on the pool when their own work spikes. The routing engine already runs every day for its first operator.

04 · WHAT EXISTS

The engine already runs.

Built for one operator's own accounts and GPUs, used every day, and split so anyone can run it.

  • Routing proxy

    Go. Sits beside Claude Code, Codex and other tools, and routes each request by model class.

  • Failover

    On a limit or an error it retries elsewhere before your tool notices.

  • The brain

    Python. Reads each source's usage and limits and picks who serves what.

  • Local models

    A model on your own GPU is one more source in the same picker.

  • Self-host edition

    Two containers and one setup command, tested end to end on every change.

The engine is real

  • class=frontier → your account · 200
  • class=fast → your account · 200
  • plan at limit → pool GPU · qwen3-coder-30b · 200
  • pool GPU busy → another member's GPU · 200
  • class=local → your GPU · 127.0.0.1 · 200

05 · ECONOMICS

Earn on delivery. Pay by model class.

Credit for delivery only

The pool mints credit only for capacity it actually used, as the brain measured it. Nothing for pledges, no pre-mine.

Priced by model class

A frontier-class hour and a small local model's answer are different goods, so members price each class.

A ledger, not a token

Pilot credit can't be bought, sold or transferred, the way AI Horde's kudos have worked since 2022.11 No token exists; none will be issued before counsel signs off.

08 · RISKS

What could go wrong,
and what we do about it.

RiskWhat we do

The pool carries only capacity whose terms allow it: members' GPUs running open-weight models, and capacity a provider approves. Your own plans and keys serve only your own work.

Why

Subscription plans can't be made available to anyone else,14 API keys can't be transferred to a third party,15 and startup credits can't be passed on to end customers.16 Anthropic also bars routing plan credentials on behalf of other users.1

Receipts signed by both sides, random spot checks and reputation; hardware attestation as it matures.

Why

A GPU owner and a requester could collude to mint credit for work that never happened.

No token without counsel. Pilot credit can't be bought, sold or transferred.

Why

Credit that can be traded for money may count as a security.

A pooled request runs on another member's GPU. You choose which work may burst; the brain logs metadata only.

Why

Whoever serves a request sees its content, as any inference provider does. The logging policy is written down before the first outside member joins.

Credit only for delivery, and no signup rewards.

Why

Free capacity attracts load that never pays back: Chutes processed 45% fewer tokens in six weeks once it cut subsidized use.13

Members across time zones, GPU owners with different peaks, per-member budgets.

Why

Correlated demand erodes the pooling benefit. Try "Overlap: High" in the demo above.

Routing per model class: you decide what may burst, and to what.

Why

A 30B open model is a step down for some work.
