the loop

Contribute. Earn. Draw.

Individual capacity is bursty — subscriptions idle all week then hit their ceiling mid-crunch; GPUs hum at 4% duty cycle. Pooled, the peaks cancel out. Tokens keep score of who smoothed whom.

01

Contribute

Bring a box that serves open models, or a plan or key its provider lets you pool. The pool's brain meters delivered capacity — the router and the meter are the same process, so there's no self-reported number to game.

02

Earn

Contribution mints your stake — earned on delivery, never on pledge. Governance of the pool (which sources join, how lanes are priced) belongs to the members who feed it.

03

Draw

Crunch week? Your tooling transparently rides pooled capacity, per model lane — heavy lanes burst onto the pool while light lanes stay home. If the pool vanishes, everything degrades to exactly your own setup. It can never break you.
